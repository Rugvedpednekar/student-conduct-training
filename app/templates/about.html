About This Website

This Student Conduct Training Portal was built for training purposes and is designed to support onboarding and ongoing development for Graduate Assistants in Student Conduct.

Created By

This website was created by the Graduate Assistant in Student Conduct for the 2025–2026 academic year.

Purpose

The portal was developed to provide structured guidance that supports Graduate Assistant training, strengthens consistency in office processes, and makes Student Conduct workflows easier to learn, reference, and follow in daily practice.

What This Website Supports

  • Organized process guidance for case handling and hearing preparation.
  • Sanctions-related workflow references and follow-up support.
  • Systems training and role-specific operational expectations.
  • A central location for important procedures, standards, and reference information.
Why It Was Built

By bringing key resources into one place, this portal helps new and returning Graduate Assistants build confidence, reduce training variability, and contribute to Student Conduct operations with greater clarity and consistency.

Appreciation

Thank you to the Student Conduct team and campus partners who support Graduate Assistant development through mentorship, collaboration, and shared standards of student-centered service.

Student-Services Commitment

The intent of this website is to help staff deliver fair, consistent, and educationally focused conduct processes that support student success.

About This Training Website

This website was created to support Student Conduct Graduate Assistant training through clearer structure, practical guidance, and a more organized learning experience for future GAs.

Website Focus Training, Guidance & Continuity

A central resource designed to make training more accessible, structured, and easier to follow.

A Message to the Next Student Conduct GA

Hi, Rugved here.

I was the Graduate Assistant in Student Conduct during the 2025–2026 academic year. First of all, congratulations on your new role and your new office. Take time to settle in, make yourself comfortable, and get to know the people around you. Meet your colleagues across DSS, because you will be spending a lot of time working with them, learning from them, and growing with them.

Why I Created This Website

I created this website to help future Student Conduct Graduate Assistants with their training and learning process. When I first joined the office, most of the training materials were limited to documents and two Maxient videos. It took me a long time to understand the workflows, expectations, and daily responsibilities.

I wanted to improve that experience for the next person stepping into this role. That is why I built this website. My goal was to make the training process more structured, easier to follow, and more practical for day-to-day work.

Purpose of This Website

This portal was created for training purposes and is meant to serve as a central place for guidance, process flow, and reference material. I tried to organize it in a way that would make the learning curve smoother and help future GAs understand not only what to do, but also how the work connects across the office.

A Personal Note

This website is my gift to the Department of Student Conduct and to Dave. I genuinely enjoyed my time in this office, and I hope you do too.

I hope this website helps make your transition into the role easier, gives you confidence as you learn, and supports you throughout your time here.

All the Best
